📋 Employee Performance & Accountability Scorecards

Calculated live from the task sheet: Score = (0.6 × OnTime%) + (0.4 × Completion%) − (5 × RedFlags)

On-Time % = completed on time ÷ completed tasks that have a due date. If an employee has no dated completions, the score uses Completion % alone. Red flags = sum of the Red Flags column on that employee's tasks. Score is capped 0–100.

1. Employee Name 2. Department 3. Assigned Manager 4. Tasks Assigned 5. Completed 6. On-Time % 7. Avg Days to Close 8. Red Flags 9. Performance Score
